    What do these words mean?
    EEG
    A recording of electrical activity from the brain, taken with sensors on the head.
    Channel
    One sensor’s signal. Each row on the map is one channel.
    Window
    A short slice of time (5 seconds here). Each square on the map is one channel in one window.
    Lost contact / flat line
    The sensor stopped touching the skin, so the signal went dead flat.
    Electrical hum
    Interference from mains power or a bad connection. It looks like a thick, fuzzy band instead of a line.
    Spikes and swings
    Sudden jumps, usually from movement or a loose headband.
    Rule
    A condition you set, like “flag any window where the signal goes flat for more than half of it”.
