Is everyone wearing the headband properly?

Drop in the recordings from your participants, one file per night. You get one row per night: who’s fine, who needs a follow-up, what went wrong, and what to tell them. Nothing is uploaded; the files are read on your computer.

ParticipantStatusClean signalWhat happenedWhat to do

“Worn properly” means the headband gave a clean signal for at least 75% of the night. 50–74% is “Check this one”. Below 50% is “Not worn properly”. These are the same cut-offs a 2026 home-sleep study used.

What do these words mean?
Clean signal
The share of the night when the headband was recording a usable brain signal.
Lost contact / flat line
The sensor stopped touching the skin, so the signal went dead flat.
Electrical hum
Interference from mains power or a bad connection. It looks like a thick, fuzzy band instead of a line.
Spikes and swings
Sudden jumps, usually from movement or a loose headband.
EDF file
The standard file format sleep and EEG devices export. Most headbands and sleep apps can export it.
